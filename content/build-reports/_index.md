The core capability of Workday OfficeConnect is pulling live Adaptive Planning or Financials data into Excel. Learn how to use the Reporting pane, add accounts and time elements, filter data, and manage report properties.

## Where to start

- **New to OfficeConnect?** Start with the [Reporting Pane Tour](/build-reports/reporting-pane-tour/), then [Build Your First Report](/build-reports/build-first-report/).
- **Ready for FP&A scenarios?** Try [Rolling 12-Month Report](/build-reports/rolling-12-month-report/), [Budget vs Actuals Variance](/build-reports/budget-vs-actuals-variance/), or [Department P&L Report](/build-reports/department-pl-report/).
- **Reporting on general-ledger actuals?** Jump into the dedicated [Financials Reports](/build-reports/financials/) sub-section for trial balance, balance sheet, cash flow, drill-through, intercompany, multi-currency, and the month-end close workflow.
- **Working in Word or PowerPoint?** Cross over to [Word & PowerPoint](/word-powerpoint/) for board books and linked decks.
- **Submitting data back to Adaptive Planning?** See [Data Entry & Write-Back](/data-entry-writeback/).
